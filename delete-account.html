Delete your account and data

Applies to the app World of Gems by The Verdict Company (Patrick Schwartmann).

Option 1: Directly in the app (immediately)

  1. In the app, open ⚙️ Settings (gear icon at the top).
  2. At the bottom, in the “Danger zone” section, tap “Delete account & data”.
  3. Confirm the security prompt with “Yes, delete permanently”.

Your account is deleted immediately and irreversibly.

Option 2: Request by email

If you no longer have access to the app, send an email from the email address of your game account to TheVerdictCompany@gmail.com with the subject “Account deletion World of Gems” and your player name. We will delete your account within 7 days and confirm the deletion to you.

Which data is deleted

When you delete your account, all data stored for your account is permanently removed:

  • Sign-in data (email address, and name and profile picture link if you signed in with Google or Facebook) and player name
  • Save game including inventory and the coordinates of your mines (including all claims visible on the map)
  • Your most recent position stored for the anti-cheat check and all anti-cheat records
  • Chat messages and private messages, friends list and friend requests
  • Leaderboard entries, market orders and offers, guild membership (guild leadership is handed over or the guild is dissolved)
  • Support tickets, stop suggestions and reports, purchase records
  • Game and page statistics, daily activity, consent and safety log, and push notification registrations

Items you have already delivered to other players through the market remain with those players.

Retention

We do not keep your data beyond this. The only exception are copies in our technical backups, which are automatically overwritten or deleted after 35 days at the latest. Deleted accounts are not restored from backups. Crash reports stored by Google Firebase Crashlytics are deleted automatically after 90 days.

Questions?

If you have any questions about deletion, contact us at TheVerdictCompany@gmail.com (see also the Privacy Policy and the Legal Notice).
