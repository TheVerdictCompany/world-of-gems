Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026 · applies to the Android app World of Gems (published on Google Play by “The Verdict Company”, Patrick Schwartmann) and to this website.

In short

  • We process the data needed to run the game: your account, your game progress, chat and the coordinates of the mines you build.
  • Your GPS position is used on your device. It is only sent to our server when you perform a location-based action (for example collecting, building, scanning, auto-collect) and when the stops around you are loaded – for the game to work and to protect against cheating. We keep only your most recent position, for at most 30 days.
  • Mines you build are shown on the map to other players together with your player name. Do not build mines at places you do not want to share (for example your home).
  • We show optional rewarded video ads (Google AdMob); in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland only after you have made your choice in the consent dialog. We use Google Firebase for push notifications and crash reports. We do not use Firebase Analytics.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data in the app at any time (how it works).

2. Which data we process and why

2.1 Account and sign-in

  • Player name (chosen by you) and email address.
  • You can sign in with Google, Facebook or an email link (“magic link”). If you use Google or Facebook, the provider sends us your email address and, depending on your settings there, your name and a link to your profile picture. These are stored by our sign-in service; we do not display them in the game and do not use them for any other purpose.
  • A random device ID generated by the app (not your hardware ID), the sign-in method, the app version you use and when you were last active – to prevent duplicate accounts, to provide support and to tell you about required updates.
  • Language: the language you selected in the app (German/English), so that push notifications from our server arrive in your language.
  • You can also play as a guest. In that case your game data stays on your device only.

2.2 Game progress

Your save game (progress, inventory, buildings, settings) is stored in your cloud account so that you do not lose your progress and can continue on another device. A copy is kept locally on your device.

2.3 Location

  • The map and location-based features only work after you have consented to location use in the app and granted Android's location permission (“while using the app”). The game does not use your location in the background.
  • Your live position is used on your device to centre the map and to check whether you are close enough to a deposit, stop or other game object. It is not sent to our server continuously.
  • Anti-cheat check: when you perform a location-based action (for example building a mine, collecting at a stop, scanning, collecting meteorites or event finds, collecting automatically while “auto-collect” is running), the app sends your current coordinates, the GPS accuracy, your speed and the type of action to our server – at most once every 10 seconds per action type. The server stores only the most recent position (each new one overwrites the previous one), for at most 30 days, and compares it with the previous one to detect impossible “jumps”. If the app detects a fake GPS app (mock location) or an impossible jump, we record the event (time, distance, speed, accuracy – no coordinates) and location-based actions may be locked temporarily (currently for 7 days). You will then receive a message in the support inbox.
  • Stops nearby: to show the stops around you, the app queries our server with the centre of the map when the map is loaded. This query is not stored.
  • Mine claims (visible to other players): when you build a mine, its coordinates are stored together with your player name and shown to other players on the map (“one deposit – one owner”). These places may reveal where you have been. Claims are removed automatically when the lease expires (currently 3 days, unless you extend it).
  • If you suggest a new stop or report a place as inaccessible, we store the coordinates, the name and your note together with your account in order to review your report.
  • To display the map and places nearby, your device loads map tiles from MapTiler and, if needed, place data from the public OpenStreetMap Overpass API. These services receive your IP address and the map area that is being loaded (which corresponds roughly to your surroundings).
  • You can withdraw your consent at any time in the app (Settings › Location) and in Android's settings. Without location consent, map features are not available; the rest of the game remains playable.

2.4 Chat, private messages, friends and guilds

  • Public chat rooms (German, English, Global, Trade): your messages are stored together with your player name, guild tag and visible badges and can be read by other players.
  • Guild chat: visible to the members of your guild.
  • Private messages are only possible between accepted friends and are only visible to you and the recipient. To enforce the chat rules and to protect players, administrators and moderators can access messages, including private messages, and delete them.
  • Messages are checked automatically when sent: links are blocked and certain offensive words are replaced. Messages are limited to 300 characters. Accounts that break the rules can be suspended.
  • Reporting and blocking: you can report messages and players. For a report we store who reported, which player or message it concerns, the reason, your note, a copy of the reported message text with its time and a short conversation context, and how moderation handled it (outcome, note, who handled it). This also applies to reported private messages – they are then shown to the moderators for review. If you block a player, we store this for as long as the block exists; an existing friendship ends.
  • Translation for moderation (DeepL): so that our moderators can assess messages written in other languages, they can have individual messages from public rooms and guild chats translated by DeepL SE (Cologne, Germany). Only the message text is transmitted – without player names or other account data; private messages are never translated. We cache the translation for at most 30 days. We use the free version of the DeepL API (DeepL API Free). Under this version, DeepL may, according to its own terms, temporarily store the submitted texts and use them to improve its services; details are set out in DeepL's privacy policy. Please therefore do not share personal data (e.g. your address or phone number) in public or guild chats. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in effective moderation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • If someone mentions you with “@name”, we store a short notification (who mentioned you and a snippet of the message) for you.
  • Friends: friend requests and connections (player name of the other person). Friend codes for invitations, if you use them.
  • Guild: membership, role, contributions to guild missions, donations and the guild activity log are visible to the members of your guild.

2.5 Profile, leaderboards and trading

Your player name, level, score, titles, avatar, frame, background, guild and achievements are shown to other players on your profile and in leaderboards. Orders and offers you place in the in-game market are visible to other players together with your player name.

2.6 Support

If you send a support ticket, we store your message, the category, your player name and our reply so that we can help you and you can see the answer in the app.

2.7 Notifications

  • Reminders on your device (for example “storage full” or “lease ending”) are scheduled locally; no data is sent to our server for this.
  • Push notifications (for example for a new private message, game events or a reminder after a longer break) are sent via Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google). For this, we store a push token of your device linked to your account. For private messages, the notification contains the sender's player name and the first 120 characters of the message, which are transmitted through Google's servers.
  • You can choose the notification level in the app (Off / Important only / All) and revoke the permission in Android's settings at any time.

2.8 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app offers optional rewarded video ads: you decide whether you want to watch an ad in exchange for an in-game reward. Ads are delivered by Google AdMob. If you are located in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before the first ad using Google's consent dialog (Google UMP, IAB TCF standard). If you consent, Google may also use your device's advertising ID, your IP address, device information and your interaction with the ad for personalised advertising. If you do not consent, you only receive non-personalised or limited ads; even then, Google processes the data technically required to deliver ads, limit how often they are shown, measure them and prevent fraud. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings › “Ad privacy settings”; you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. We store how many ads you have watched per day to apply the daily limit.

2.9 In-app purchases

Purchases are processed exclusively by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. We receive and store the product purchased, the order ID, the purchase token and the time of purchase, and we verify the purchase with Google in order to credit it to your account and to prevent fraud.

2.10 Game statistics (balancing and app improvement)

  • Game metrics: about every 5 minutes while you play, the app sends a snapshot of game figures (for example level, in-game money, number of mines, items sold, completed contracts) – without location data. These snapshots are linked to your account (pseudonymous, not anonymous).
  • Page statistics: which screens and tabs of the app you open per day, how often and for how long (in time ranges), and from which screen you switch to which. No content and no location data. Sent in bundles at most every 5 minutes, linked to your account.
  • Daily activity and event log: on which days your account was active and a log of your game events (for example building, production, sales) for player statistics, event tiers and resolving support cases.

2.11 Crash and error reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes or a technical error occurs, a report is sent to Google Firebase Crashlytics: technical details of the error, device model, Android version, app version, a Crashlytics installation ID, and – to help us answer support requests – your account ID (no player name, no email address). Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) is disabled in the app; it does not collect usage data or the advertising ID.

2.12 Consent and safety log

We record when you grant or refuse location consent, when you confirm the safety notice shown at app start, and when you confirm that you are not driving (speed lock), together with the time and technical device information (user agent), in order to be able to prove these confirmations.

2.13 Technical logs and security

When the app communicates with our server, your IP address and technical connection data are processed and briefly logged by our hosting provider in order to deliver the service and to protect it against misuse. Some program libraries (map display, database client) are loaded from the public content delivery networks unpkg.com and cdn.jsdelivr.net; these receive your IP address.

2.14 This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc.). When you visit it, GitHub processes your IP address and technical access data to deliver the pages. We do not use cookies or tracking on this website.

2.15 Data stored on your device

The app stores your save game, your settings (for example language, location consent, map layers) and a random device ID in the app's local storage on your device. You can remove this data by signing out, clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

3. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

If the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the UK GDPR applies to you, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): account, sign-in, save game, mine claims, chat, friends, guilds, leaderboards, market, support, push notifications you have enabled, in-app purchases, language for push notifications.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; for access to your device also Section 25(1) of the German TDDDG): use of your location; push notifications (via Android's permission); personalised advertising via the consent dialog. You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): anti-cheat checks and fair play, security and abuse prevention, chat moderation including reports and translation for moderation, protection of other players, crash reports, game and page statistics for balancing and improving the app, non-personalised advertising to finance the free game, and keeping records of confirmations. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests (see section 7).
  • Legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR): for example responding to requests from authorities.

6. Security

All connections between the app and our server are encrypted (HTTPS). Access to data is restricted at database level (row-level security): players can only access their own data and the information intended to be visible to other players. Administrative access is limited to the operator and appointed moderators.
